Privacy Policy

Effective and reviewed: November 6, 2026

This policy describes information handled by the ZAQORUM Android app, which creates visual representations inspired by ancient writing systems.

Entered text and saved artwork

The reviewed conversion engine runs locally; no transfer of entered names or phrases to a ZAQORUM conversion server was found. If you save a creation, its source text, result, design details and timestamps are stored in local SQLite. You may delete saved creations in the app. Usage attempts and some advertising state are stored in SharedPreferences. The reviewed app contains no ZAQORUM account, analytics SDK, or crash-reporting SDK.

Android backup

Android backup is enabled and includes the app database and shared preferences for cloud backup and device transfer. Saved artwork and some preferences or usage state may therefore be included, depending on Android and Google account settings. Android/Google control retention; deleting an item in-app does not necessarily remove an existing backup immediately.

Google Mobile Ads and UMP

When ad requests are allowed by consent, Google Mobile Ads may collect or share IP address (potentially used to infer general location), app/ad interactions, diagnostic and performance information, and identifiers such as Advertising ID or App Set ID when available. Google uses these data for advertising, measurement/analytics, fraud prevention and service operation under its documentation and settings. UMP refreshes consent information, shows a form when required, and manages privacy choices. The app checks canRequestAds() before ads initialization/request. Processing depends on consent, region, settings and ad configuration.

Google Fonts

The bundled interface requests fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. The request sends the IP address and network information needed to fetch fonts to Google; WebView may cache them.

Sharing, retention and deletion

Images are generated on-device. If you share, Android hands the selected image or text to the destination app, whose own policy applies. Saved artwork remains until deleted, app data is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Backups and Google ad/consent records may persist under their providers' policies; ZAQORUM does not control Google's retention.

Children and contact

This policy does not declare the app child-directed or not child-directed. The app owner must set the target audience in Play Console and AdMob before release. For privacy questions email zaqorum.app@gmail.com.

This policy describes the inspected implementation but does not replace the Play Console Data safety declaration. The owner must confirm target age group and AdMob/UMP messages.
